Questions

Before you write to us.

React Native or Flutter?

We work with both. If you already have a codebase, we keep it. For a new app we recommend one based on your team and features, and explain why in the quote.

Do you do calls?

We work by written chat. It keeps a clear record of what was agreed and avoids misunderstandings across time zones.

Who owns the code and the store accounts?

You do. We work in your repository and your developer accounts, and hand over every key and setting.

Can you take over an existing app?

Yes. Send the repository and a list of what you need. We review it and reply with a plan and a fixed quote.
